Delete your LANDZOOMIX data

Last updated: 6 October 2026.

You can delete everything LANDZOOMIX keeps about you, with or without the app.

In the app (fastest)

Open LANDZOOMIX → Settings → Delete my data → confirm. This deletes, at once:

  • your progress on this phone (levels, coins, streak, medals, quests, settings);
  • the backup of your progress in the cloud;
  • your LANDZOOMIX account (the anonymous account, and the link to Google or Apple if you made one);
  • the app's analytics identifier and any unsent crash reports.

The game then starts fresh. A "Remove ads" purchase belongs to your store account and can be restored at any time.

Without the app

If the app is no longer installed, write to landzoomix@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data", and tell us:

  • the Google or Apple account you linked to LANDZOOMIX, if you linked one; or
  • if you never linked an account, roughly when you last played and on which phone (we will explain how to find the account id, if needed).

We confirm the deletion within 30 days. Progress that was never linked to an account cannot be tied to you; it is deleted automatically after 12 months without play.
