2. Your account and the cloud backup

  • Anonymous account. The first time you play, the App creates an anonymous account (a random id, no name, no email). There is no sign-in screen.
  • What is backed up. Your game progress: levels, best scores, coins, streak, medals, weekly quests, Daily Mix steps and settings. Device-only data (your reminder schedule, ad and review prompt counters) stays on the phone.
  • Where. Google Cloud Firestore, in the European Union (europe-west1, Belgium), readable only by your account. Your phone keeps its own signed copy, with daily backups.
  • Linking (optional). "Protect your progress" or "Restore my progress" links your Google or Apple account, so your progress comes back on any phone. Firebase Authentication then stores that account's id and email address. Progress from two phones is combined; nothing is lost.
  • How long. Until you delete it, or 12 months after you last played: an untouched backup is deleted automatically.
  • Deleting it. Settings → Delete my data deletes the cloud backup, your account and the data on your phone, at once. Without the App: https://ayoubajaani.github.io/landzoomix-legal/delete-data.html.

3. Advertising and consent

The App is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent with Google's consent tool before any ad is requested, and Firebase Analytics stays off until then. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & ad choices; US residents can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal data there too, where the law provides it.

On iOS we ask once for permission to track (from your third session). If you decline, you see non-personalised ads and the game is exactly the same.

Rewarded ads are only ever shown when you ask for one (a hint, a revive, double coins). Buying Remove ads removes banners and interstitial ads; rewarded ads stay available on request.

4. Children

The App is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

5. Retention

Game data stays on your device until you delete the App or use Settings → Delete my data. The cloud backup and your account are kept until you delete them, or 12 months after you last played. Analytics data is kept for 14 months and crash reports for 90 days, per our providers' settings.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, LGPD), you can request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection and portability, and withdraw your consent. Settings → Delete my data erases your cloud backup, your account, the data on your device and the App's analytics identifier, then opens an email to ask us to delete the copies held by our other providers; you can also use https://ayoubajaani.github.io/landzoomix-legal/delete-data.html or write to landzoomix@gmail.com. You can complain to your local data protection authority.

7. International transfers

Our providers may process data outside your country (for example in the USA) under appropriate safeguards such as the Standard Contractual Clauses.
